What is the lightweight division?

Lightweight is the 155 lb (70 kg) weight class in the UFC, and a lot of fans think it is the deepest division in the sport.

Who is champion right now?

Justin Gaethje beat Ilia Topuria in June 2026 to become the undisputed champion.
