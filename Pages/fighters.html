The champion

Justin Gaethje is the undisputed champion after beating Ilia Topuria in June 2026. He is known for wild, exciting fights.

Names to watch

  • Ilia Topuria: the former champion, who lost for the first time in his career against Gaethje.
  • Paddy Pimblett: lost to Gaethje in the interim title fight at UFC 324.
  • Arman Tsarukyan: one of the top contenders in the division.
  • Charles Oliveira: former champion with the most finishes in UFC history.
  • Max Holloway: former featherweight champion now competing at 155 lb.
